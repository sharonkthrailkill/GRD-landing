Greenville Roller Derby (GRD) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit sports league based in Greenville, South Carolina. This domain, greenvillerollerderby.org, is GRD's official email and administrative domain.

Greenville Roller Derby provides a fun and welcoming environment where all people have opportunities to train their bodies and minds through athletic competition and community service. We strive to nurture and empower our members to become valuable players, teammates, and community members both on and off the track.

Greenville Roller Derby is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 90-0586606) and a proud member of the Women's Flat Track Derby Association.
